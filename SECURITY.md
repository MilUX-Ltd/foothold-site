# Security

If you find a security problem with the Foothold site, please tell us at **contact@milux.co.uk**. Put "Security" in the subject line, and describe what you found and how to see it. Please do not publish it until we have had a chance to fix it.

We will reply within five working days, tell you what we are doing about it, and credit you if you would like.

The Foothold site has no accounts, sets no cookies and runs no script of its own. It has no bug bounty. The reasoning is in [`docs/security/threat-model.md`](docs/security/threat-model.md).
